Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

StreakFC is a soccer training app for kids, their families and their coaches. We collect as little as possible, we never sell data, and we never show ads.

What we collect

  • Players: a first name or nickname, an age group, and training activity (touches, sessions, streaks, levels, check-ins and homework a coach assigns). We do not ask players for last names, email, phone numbers, photos, school or location.
  • Parents and coaches: an email address to sign in, and a display name.
  • Devices: a random sign-in token stored on the phone or tablet so the player stays logged in, and push notification settings if you turn them on.

How we use it

  • To run the app: track training, keep streaks, show progress, and let a player's coach see their team's training.
  • Team leaderboards show first names or nicknames and training numbers to teammates only. A parent can turn leaderboards off.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information with advertisers. We do not use it for advertising or behavioral profiling.

Who can see a player's data

  • The player, and a parent who connects to the player's account.
  • Coaches of the teams or programs the player joined, for those teams only. Coaches never see parent contact information.
  • StreakFC staff, to run and support the service.
  • Service providers that host the app for us (for example our database and web hosting), under their own security commitments. Data is stored in the United States.

Children

StreakFC is built for youth players. Players under 13 should use StreakFC with a parent's account or through a team their coach runs. We only collect what is needed to run the app (a first name or nickname, an age group and training activity), and parents can connect to their child's account at any time. If you are a parent and believe your child gave us information you did not want them to, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Parents can view, change or delete a player's information, leave any team or program, and turn off leaderboards and notifications.
  • To delete an account or a player, email us and we will delete it within 30 days.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is limited by account, team and role. PINs are stored hashed. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell affected users if a breach happens.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and let users know in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: hello@streakfc.app. StreakFC LLC, Arizona, USA.
